# Privacy Policy for Clamera

**Last Updated: September 4, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Clamera ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Clamera mobile application ("App," "Service"). By using Clamera, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Username
- Password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your plaintext password)

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and profile information as provided by Google's authentication service.

### Profile & User Content
- Profile picture (uploaded by you, or provided via Google Sign-In)
- Fish catch photos, species names, descriptions, and weights that you submit
- Aquarium customization data (fish placement, settings)
- Username and any username changes you make

### Photos Submitted for AI Species Identification
When you use Clamera's fish identification feature, the photo you submit is sent to Google's Gemini AI service for analysis, in order to identify the species shown. This photo is processed by Google's Gemini API and is not stored by us beyond what is necessary to complete the identification request.

### Usage & App Activity Data
- Clam Points balance and transaction history (earned, spent, gifted)
- Friends list, friend requests, and social interactions within the App
- Login streak and activity dates
- Daily usage counts for AI-powered features (used solely to enforce fair usage limits)

### Advertising Data
Clamera displays advertisements through Google Mobile Ads, including rewarded video ads. Google may collect device identifiers and interaction data as described in Google's own privacy policy, in order to serve and measure ads.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and maintain your account
- Enable core app features (catching, cataloging, and displaying fish; social features like friends and gifting)
- Identify fish species from submitted photos using AI
- Display advertisements and reward you for engaging with them
- Enforce fair usage limits and prevent abuse
- Communicate with you about your account or respond to support requests
- Improve and maintain the App

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Third-Party Services

Clamera relies on the following third-party services, each of which processes certain data on our behalf:

- **Firebase (Google)** — authentication, database storage, cloud functions, and file storage
- **Google Sign-In** — optional account authentication
- **Google Mobile Ads** — advertisement delivery and measurement
- **Google Gemini API** — AI-based fish species identification from submitted photos

Each of these services has its own privacy practices, governed by Google's Privacy Policy, which you can review at [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Firebase, a Google Cloud service, with industry-standard security practices. Access to backend systems (including API keys and secrets) is restricted and never exposed within the app itself. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

We retain your account information and user content for as long as your account remains active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your associated data within a reasonable time, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes.

## Your Rights & Choices

You can:
- Update your profile information, username, and password (for email/password accounts) directly within the App
- Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below
- Report content or other users that violate our Terms of Service

## Children's Privacy

Clamera is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

## International Users

If you use Clamera from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers (including Firebase and Google) operate.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
